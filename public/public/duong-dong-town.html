Duong Dong Town: An Introduction to Phu Quoc's Main Settlement

Around 70,000 people live in the main settlement of Phu Quoc, and much of the island's daily life plays out on its streets. Fish are auctioned at dawn, school traffic fills the roads around 7 AM, and café conversations carry on into the afternoon.

Good to know

For travellers, the town works in two ways. It is a destination in itself, with a night market, temples, beaches and fish sauce factories to explore. It is also the island's practical hub, where you find many of the transport connections, banks, pharmacies, SIM card shops and motorbike rental operators.

Conclusion

Minh hoạ: duong dong town

Duong Dong Town rewards a slower pace. Visitors who treat it purely as a transit point tend to miss its quieter cultural corners, such as the old pagoda on Hung Vuong Street or the small temple watching over the river mouth. Spending even one unhurried day here gives a clearer picture of how islanders actually live. For more, see the an overview of Duong Dong town.
