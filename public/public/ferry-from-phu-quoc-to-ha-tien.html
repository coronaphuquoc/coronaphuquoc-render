Spending sensibly on the crossing

Keeping costs in check on this route starts with matching the service to your needs. Fares vary by operator and seat class, and children and senior reductions apply on all four lines, so it is worth confirming current rates directly with the operator at the time of booking. If you are not carrying a vehicle, a passenger-only service is the leaner choice.

Good to know

A few practical habits help:

  • Carry only what you need, since vehicle transport is a separate charge on Thanh Thoi and Binh An
  • Book online through operator websites or platforms such as Traveloka, Vexere, or Baolau to compare times in one view
  • Travel on a weekday outside Tet and public holidays, when demand is lower
