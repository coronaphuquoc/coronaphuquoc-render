A Fish Noodle Breakfast Run Along 30 Thang 4 Street

Some of the most characteristic bowls on the island are served on one short stretch of 30 Thang 4 Street in Duong Dong. A morning walk there can cover two very different fish-based noodle soups before the heat builds.

Bun ken at Ken Ut Luom

Bun ken is the quieter cousin of the better known island dishes. The broth is thick and golden, made from finely ground fish such as barracuda or yellowtail scad, softened with coconut milk and a light trace of curry. The result is slightly sweet and fragrant. The address given is 42 30 Thang 4 Street, open from 6:00 AM to midday, and the shop often sells out early. A bowl is listed at about 35,000 VND.

Squid noodle soup nearby

At Hu Tieu Muc Tu Huong the broth is simmered from bones and fresh squid, giving a clear and naturally sweet soup with tender squid rings. It is a lighter choice for a second stop. Listed prices run around 40,000 to 70,000 VND a bowl.

Crab noodle soup to finish

Quoc Anh Crab Noodle Soup is also on 30/4 Street. Its brick-red broth with firm crab meat and chewy noodles is heavier, so it suits a late breakfast or early lunch.

  • Start with bun ken before it runs out.
  • Share bowls if you want to taste all three.
  • Carry small notes for paying at street shops.
  • Check prices on the day, as they can shift.

Conclusion

Three bowls, one street and under two hours gives a clear picture of how the island cooks fish. Further reading: local dishes and where to eat them.
