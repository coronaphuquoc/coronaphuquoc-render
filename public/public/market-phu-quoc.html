An honest introduction to island trading

To experience everyday Phu Quoc, the markets are a good place to begin. Across the island, eight stand out, and together they reflect local life clearly. Some are morning seafood markets, others evening street-food stretches, and a few serve mainly residents. Duong Dong Market in the town center trades fresh seafood and spices. Ham Ninh Market on the east coast is known for steamed crab and sea urchin. The Phu Quoc Night Market in Duong Dong leans toward street food and souvenirs, while Grand World Night Market at Bai Dai mixes entertainment with photo opportunities. Ganh Dau, An Thoi and Cau Sau round out the picture with rural and portside trade. Thinking about which Market Phu Quoc to visit, it helps to match the place to your interest: fresh produce by day, food and souvenirs by night, or a quiet look at ordinary island routines.
