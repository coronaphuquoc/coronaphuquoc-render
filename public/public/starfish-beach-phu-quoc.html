The road north from Duong Dong to Rach Vem

Starfish Beach Phu Quoc lies about 25 kilometers north of Duong Dong, the main town. Under normal conditions the drive takes 45 to 50 minutes. Searching "Rach Vem Fishing Village" on Google Maps gives accurate directions.

Good to know

The first 20 kilometers follow a paved coastal road in good condition. The final 5 kilometers are a bumpy red-earth track, so drive slowly and watch for potholes. In the dry season this stretch is manageable for any vehicle type. In heavy rain it can turn muddy, and a 45-minute trip may stretch past an hour.

Practical tips

There are three common ways to travel:

  • a rented motorbike, which lets you stop at pepper farms or viewpoints
  • a private car or taxi, more comfortable for families and groups
  • an organised tour that bundles transport and a local guide
