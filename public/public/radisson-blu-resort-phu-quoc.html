Rooms, Suites and Pool Villas at Radisson Blu Phu Quoc Explained

Picking a room category is where most planning time goes. Radisson Blu Resort Phu Quoc has 514 rooms, suites and pool villas, so it helps to understand how the options differ before you scroll through a booking page.

Rooms and suites

Every room and suite comes with a private balcony facing the ocean, the mountains or the gardens. In-room features include Wi-Fi and quality bedding. The interior style blends Scandinavian simplicity with tropical touches, giving a light and quiet atmosphere.

  • Deluxe Rooms: a practical choice for couples and solo travellers
  • Suites: more living space for longer stays
  • Views: ocean, mountain or garden, depending on category

Pool villas for families and friends

The beachfront villas are designed as a home away from home. They have multiple bedrooms, spacious living and dining areas, fully equipped kitchens and private pools with direct access to the sand. That combination works well for families who want to cook simple breakfasts or groups who like to gather in one place.

How to choose

Think about how much time you will spend in the room. If you plan to be out exploring all day, a Deluxe Room may be enough. If you want slow mornings and long evenings together, the villa space earns its keep.

  • Out all day: a Deluxe Room
  • Couple wanting space: a Suite
  • Family or friends: a pool villa

A small tip on views

A sea-view balcony matters most at sunset on this west-facing coast. Garden views are quieter and often shadier in the afternoon.

Conclusion

From balconied rooms to pool villas with kitchens, the resort covers most travel styles. Match the category to how you actually travel. Further reading: room and villa options on Bai Dai.
