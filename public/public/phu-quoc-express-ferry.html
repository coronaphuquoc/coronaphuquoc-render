Reading the Phu Quoc Express Timetable Without Confusion

Departure times on the Phu Quoc Express look simple until you try to fit them around a bus from Ho Chi Minh City or a border crossing. Here is the published schedule laid out by route, with notes on how to use it. Times can be revised, so confirm when booking.

Ha Tien outbound and return

  • 7:00 AM, arriving 8:15 AM
  • 9:00 AM, arriving 10:15 AM
  • 11:45 AM, arriving 1:00 PM
  • Return from Bai Vong: 7:00 AM, 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM, each about 75 minutes

Rach Gia outbound and return

  • 7:00 AM, arriving 9:20 AM
  • 10:20 AM, arriving 12:40 PM
  • 12:10 PM, arriving 2:30 PM
  • 1:00 PM, arriving 3:20 PM
  • Return from Bai Vong: 7:10 AM, 10:00 AM and 1:20 PM

How to plan around these times

Notice that nothing leaves in the evening. Ha Tien sailings stop in the early afternoon, so an overnight bus that arrives late morning still has the 11:45 sailing as a fallback. At Rach Gia, the terminal asks for 60 to 90 minutes before departure around holidays, while Ha Tien suggests 45 to 60 minutes. Build those margins into your road transfer rather than aiming for the last minute.

If none of these hours suit you

Minh hoạ: phu quoc express ferry

The other operator, Superdong, runs more daily departures on both routes, though its boats are single-hull. Its own system lists departure times only, so ask for the arrival time when you book.

Conclusion

Pick the sailing first, then work backwards to your bus or car pickup, and keep an hour of slack for the terminal. Further reading: full schedule and crossing guide.
