Studio Suite, Family Room or Standard: Picking a Room at VinHolidays Fiesta

VinHolidays Fiesta Phu Quoc offers three broad room groups. All share a minimalist style and essential amenities, but each fits a different kind of traveller. Here is how to tell them apart.

Studio Suite

The Studio Suite is the more spacious choice, with open views over Grand World. Amenities include a bathtub, electronic safe, flat-screen TV, hairdryer, and tea and coffee in the room. It suits travellers who want a little more comfort and privacy, such as couples on a short break.

  • Views over Grand World
  • Bathtub and in-room safe
  • Tea and coffee provided

Family Room and Family Pool View

These rooms consist of two connecting rooms. That arrangement balances togetherness and privacy: parents and children, or two generations of one family, stay side by side while keeping their own space. The Pool View version adds an outlook over the pool.

Standard rooms

The Standard Pool View Twin, Standard Twin and Standard Double cover the basics: Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a flat-screen TV, an electric kettle and a wardrobe. They suit groups of friends, small families or business travellers who want a simple, easy stay.

How to decide

  • Couple wanting comfort: Studio Suite
  • Multi-generation family: Family Room
  • Friends sharing: Standard Twin
  • Solo or work trip: Standard Double

A booking note

Connecting rooms are the key feature for families, so confirm the arrangement directly with the hotel before arrival.

Conclusion

Studio Suites add comfort, Family Rooms add connecting space, and Standard rooms keep things simple. Choose by group size first. Further reading: room categories at VinHolidays Fiesta.
