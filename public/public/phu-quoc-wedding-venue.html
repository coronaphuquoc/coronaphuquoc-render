When to Hold an Outdoor Ceremony in Phu Quoc

Timing decides more about a beach wedding than any décor choice. On Phu Quoc the calendar splits neatly into a dry stretch and a wet stretch, and knowing that rhythm helps you protect the ceremony, the photographs and your guests' travel plans.

The dry season window

November through April is the period most couples aim for. Skies are generally clear, the sea is calm and temperatures sit around 26 to 30°C. Sunset falls between roughly 17:45 and 18:20, which gives photographers a reliable golden hour to work with.

  • December and January are the busiest months for bookings.
  • March and April are quieter, with slightly later sunsets.
  • November works well for couples who want dry weather before the holiday rush.

Months to treat with caution

June through September brings the monsoon. Frequent rain and rough seas make outdoor events impractical, so any wedding in that period should rely on an indoor ballroom with an outdoor element only as an extra moment if the weather allows. Short-notice dates are easier to secure in these months, but plan a solid indoor fallback.

Planning around sunset

Schedule the vows roughly 45 minutes before sunset so the light is soft during the ceremony and the couple portraits follow naturally. Tell guests the exact start time and build in a buffer for transfers from other parts of the island.

Conclusion

Pick a dry-season date first, then fit the ceremony time around the evening light. Everything else in the timeline can be built from that anchor. Further reading: seasonal advice for island ceremonies.
