Choosing Between a Garden Villa and a Beachfront Villa in North Phu Quoc

Once you decide on a villa at Wyndham Grand Phu Quoc, the next question is which kind. The resort offers garden villas tucked into greenery and beachfront villas right on the sand, plus one six bedroom house for big gatherings. Each style suits a different rhythm of holiday.

The garden villa option

Garden villas come in two and three bedroom layouts. They have generous living space and private outdoor areas surrounded by tropical plants. Because they sit a little back from the shore, they feel sheltered and quiet, which works well for families with young children who nap in the afternoon.

  • Good for: multi-generation families and friends travelling together
  • Feel: leafy, shaded, private
  • Walk to the beach: a few minutes along resort paths

The beachfront villa option

Beachfront villas open directly onto the sand. Guests hear the waves all day and can step from the terrace to the water. Each has its own plunge pool, so there is somewhere to cool off without joining the crowd at the main pool.

  • Good for: couples, honeymooners and sea lovers
  • Feel: open, breezy, close to the shoreline

The President Beachfront Villa

For reunions or private celebrations, the six bedroom President Beachfront Villa offers wide indoor and outdoor areas on a beachfront plot. It is the choice when a whole extended family wants one roof.

Questions to ask yourself first

Will the group cook together or eat out? Do young children need a shaded garden more than sea views? How many bathrooms does everyone really need? Answering these quickly narrows the choice.

Conclusion

Garden villas give shade and calm, beachfront villas give the sea at your door, and the six bedroom house covers large groups. Read the full layout notes before booking. Further reading: guide to private villas at Wyndham Grand.
