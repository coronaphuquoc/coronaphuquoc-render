Departure Times on the Rach Gia Sea Route

Knowing the daily departure pattern makes it far easier to line up a bus, a hotel checkout or an onward plan. Here is what the operators typically publish for the Rach Gia crossing. Schedules can shift, so check the official site before travelling.

High-speed departures

Phu Quoc Express usually leaves Rach Gia at three main times each day:

  • 7:00 AM
  • 10:20 AM
  • 1:00 PM

Each run takes about two and a half hours, so a morning departure puts you on the island before lunch.

Vehicle ferry departures

Thanh Thoi Ferry typically sails twice a day:

  • 8:00 AM
  • 1:15 PM

The crossing is about 3 hours 15 minutes, which means the afternoon sailing reaches the island around late afternoon.

Building your day around the boat

If you are travelling overnight from Ho Chi Minh City, the 7:00 AM catamaran leaves little margin; the 10:20 AM sailing gives more breathing room. Drivers bringing a car or motorbike on Thanh Thoi should aim for the 8:00 AM ship to arrive in daylight with time to settle in.

Arrive early

The guide suggests being at the terminal at least 30 to 45 minutes before departure. That covers check-in and boarding without a rush, and it matters more at weekends, when queues are longer.

When the sea is rough

Actual crossing times vary with sea conditions. During rougher weather, add extra time before any connection on the island.

Conclusion
