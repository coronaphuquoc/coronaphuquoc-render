Rach Vem Village Phu Quoc with Children and Older Relatives

Families travelling across generations can enjoy the northern village at a gentle pace, since much of what it offers happens slowly and close to shore.

Good to know

Near the beach the water is clear and of a safe depth, which suits paddling and cooling off. Kayaks and SUP boards can be rented, and the activity is described as mild enough for travellers of any age.

Children usually enjoy spotting starfish in the shallows during the dry months. An adult nearby can explain why the animals belong in the water.

Practical tips

For grandparents, a seat in a floating restaurant gives a calm view of the sea and the fishing boats.

A few things to plan around:

  • Pack swimwear and a change of clothes for the shallows.
  • Rice and noodle dishes on shore suit children who prefer simple food.
  • The ride north from Duong Dong takes a while, so plan breaks.
