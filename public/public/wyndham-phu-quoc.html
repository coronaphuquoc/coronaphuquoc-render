Comparing the Deluxe Garden, Pool and Ocean View Rooms

At Wyndham Grand Phu Quoc, the Deluxe rooms share the same footprint: 42 m² with a King or Twin bed. What changes is the view. Choosing well depends on how you plan to spend your days.

Deluxe Garden View

This room looks over the resort gardens and sits close to the swimming pool and the beach. It suits travellers who want quiet greenery outside the balcony and short walks to the water. Garden-facing rooms often feel cooler and more private.

Deluxe Pool View

The Pool View room overlooks the shimmering pool, a good fit for daily relaxation. Families like it because parents can see the pool area from the balcony and children can spot the water as soon as they wake up. Expect a little more daytime activity outside.

Deluxe Ocean View

For the sea itself, the Ocean View room offers wide views over the water, with easy access to the pool and the beach. On the northern coast, evenings bring soft light over the sea, which makes the balcony a natural place to end the day.

Quick comparison

  • Quiet and green: Garden View
  • Lively and family friendly: Pool View
  • Sea and sunsets: Ocean View
  • Same size and bed choice across all three

A practical tip

If you are unsure, consider how much time you will spend on the balcony. Guests out exploring all day may not notice the view much. Guests who rest at midday tend to value the ocean or garden outlook more.

Conclusion

All three Deluxe rooms share the same space and beds; the outlook is the real decision. Choose the view that matches your daily routine. Further reading: Deluxe room views and layouts explained.
