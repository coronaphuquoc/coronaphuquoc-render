Romantic Island Hideaways for Couples

Couples heading to Phu Quoc usually want two things: privacy and a view. The island has several upscale properties built around exactly that, with villas tucked into gardens and pools that face the sea.

Properties built for quiet

Regent Phu Quoc on Long Beach is an all-suites and villas resort with private pools, designed so guests rarely feel surrounded by other people. La Veranda, inspired by a 1920s French mansion with dark woods, fine linens and mosaic tiles, has an intimate, old-world mood. Lahana Resort on a hillside offers private bungalows hidden among tropical gardens and wide ocean views.

  • Ask for a villa or bungalow away from the main pool.
  • Request a sunset-facing room on the west coast.
  • Check whether in-villa dining is available for quiet evenings.

Planning the days

Mix slow and active days. A morning on a private speedboat to quieter lagoons, a long spa afternoon and a candlelit dinner on the sand is a rhythm many couples enjoy. On Long Beach, the InterContinental has the HARNN Heritage Spa, popular for couples' treatments.

Wedding or honeymoon?

Several of these intimate properties also host beachfront ceremonies and small destination weddings. If you are planning both events, staying at the same resort keeps things simple and gives you time to rest between celebrations.

Pack light layers for air-conditioned dinners and a pair of sandals that handle sand and stone paths. Small comforts make slow days feel easier.
