Stream treks: when the water runs highest

Streams in the park run fullest in the wet season and thin out in the dry months. Paths get slippery after rain, so shoes with grip matter more than anything else you pack. Allow a half day.

Good to know

Water levels depend on recent rain, so the same stream can look very different in different months. After heavy rain, rocks and roots become slippery and crossings need care.
