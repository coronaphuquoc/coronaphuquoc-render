Meeting Ong Lang, a quiet shore on the west coast

Between the towns of Duong Dong and Cua Can, about 7 to 8 km north of Duong Dong, lies Ong Lang Beach on Phu Quoc Island. It is close enough to reach in around 20 minutes, yet it tends to stay quiet while busier shores fill up by mid-morning.

Good to know

The coastline mixes soft golden sand with natural rocky stretches. Rugged cliffs, emerald water, seaweed and coral reefs give the bay a varied look, which is why some people call it the "mini Maldives" of the island.

Practical tips

The main reasons people come are simple:

  • calm, shallow water close to shore
  • snorkelling around rocky outcrops
  • sunsets over the sea from a west-facing beach

Conclusion

There is no entrance fee, and the setting suits travellers who want space and nature more than organised activities. Expect a relaxed beach with a few restaurants and vendors rather than a busy strip. Further reading: an overview of Ong Lang Beach.
