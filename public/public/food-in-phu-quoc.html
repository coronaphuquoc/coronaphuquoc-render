Morning and evening moods

The atmosphere around island food shifts with the time of day. In the early morning, local eateries feel lively and warm as fresh noodles are made on site and the first bowls of broth go out, a good window for breakfast dishes when ingredients are at their freshest, between 6:30 AM and 9:00 AM. By evening the mood changes at the Duong Dong night market, which opens around 5:00 PM and grows busiest between 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM, when charcoal grills light up and the smell of seafood drifts through the lanes. Colourful stalls, rising steam and the hum of the crowd give the night a festive feel, while the light softens over the water as the evening settles. Tasting Food In Phu Quoc at both ends of the day shows two different sides of the same island kitchen.
