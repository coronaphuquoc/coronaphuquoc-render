Finding the right beach in Phu Quoc from Duong Dong or An Thoi

Before choosing a shore, it helps to picture the island as two halves, with Duong Dong as the usual reference point for the north and An Thoi as the hub of the south.

Good to know

Heading north from Duong Dong, Ong Lang sits about 12 kilometres away. Further up the north-western coast are Bai Dai, Vung Bau, Ganh Dau and Rach Vem, along with the rustic Cua Can area, where a river meets the sea.

The south is a separate trip. Khem Beach and Sao Beach both lie in An Thoi Ward, and An Thoi port is where speedboat tours to the archipelago leave. A cable car also crosses the sea from there to Hon Thom.

Conclusion

Travel times vary, so ask locally before setting out. Grouping beaches by direction, the north on one day and the south on another, keeps time on the road shorter and leaves more of the day for the water. For more, see the finding beaches in Phu Quoc by direction.
