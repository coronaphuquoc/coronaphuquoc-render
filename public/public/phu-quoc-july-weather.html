Swimming in July: Warm Water and Calm Mornings

Many visitors assume the rainy season means no swimming. On Phu Quoc in July, that assumption is wrong. The sea stays warm, and the mornings often offer calm, inviting water.

Water temperature

The ocean around the island averages about 29°C, or 84°F, in July. That is warm enough to stay in for a long time without feeling chilled, even on cloudy days.

When to swim

The calmest conditions usually come in the morning, before afternoon clouds and showers build. Plan your beach time from soon after breakfast until late morning. By early afternoon, a shower may arrive, so it makes sense to be out of the water by then.

  • Swim in the morning
  • Head inside when clouds start to stack up
  • Return for an evening walk if the sky clears

Where to go

Sheltered beaches in the north of the island are a good choice in July, since they tend to have calmer water. Sao Beach on the southeast is another option, with soft white sand and clear water in the morning light.

Staying safe

Some days bring rougher seas. Watch for warning flags and listen to local advice. Do not swim alone after heavy rain, when currents can change. Keep children within arm's reach in the shallows.

Comfort tips

Carry quick-drying towels and clothes, since nothing dries fast in high humidity. Use sunscreen even when it looks cloudy, because the tropical sun is strong on bright mornings.

Conclusion

With the sea at around 29°C and calm mornings common, July still offers good swimming on Phu Quoc. Get in early, choose sheltered beaches and leave the afternoons to the rain. The guide below covers more July advice. Further reading: sea temperature and swim timing in July.
