Flying into Phu Quoc and Your First Hour on the Ground

The first hour after landing sets the mood for a holiday. Knowing what waits at the terminal exit lets you move off quickly instead of standing in the heat comparing options.

The airport

Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) is the main gateway. According to the landing guide, it handles dozens of direct flights each day from Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi and Da Nang, along with international routes from hubs such as Seoul, Bangkok and Singapore. The airport sits centrally on the island, which makes onward trips shorter in most directions.

Leaving the terminal

  • Hotel pickup, if your resort offers it
  • Taxis waiting at the exit
  • Ride-hailing apps such as Grab and Xanh SM
  • VinBus routes linking the airport with Long Beach and the north

Before you land

Exchange a little Vietnamese dong at the airport for your first rides. A local SIM card with data makes it easy to book a car and follow GPS. If you arrive during a peak holiday such as Tet or Christmas, arrange your transfer ahead to avoid a long wait.

Minh hoạ: transportation in Phu Quoc

Entry paperwork

The landing guide mentions a 30 day visa exemption for foreign nationals who fly directly to the island or transit through Vietnam's international terminals. Rules can change, so confirm with an official source before travel. Keep a passport valid for at least six months and printed copies of your hotel booking.

Conclusion

A little preparation, cash, data and a booked transfer if needed, turns the arrival into a smooth start. Further reading: arrival and transfer advice for Phu Quoc.
