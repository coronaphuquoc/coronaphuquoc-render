When to plan a swim at Khem Beach

Sheltered geography means this southern bay can be swum year-round, which sets it apart from more exposed shores. Even so, each season feels different.

Good to know

  • November to April: dry weather and clear water, but the busiest period
  • May to June: fewer people, occasional afternoon rain, water still calm
  • July to September: other beaches on the island get choppy while Khem stays calm
  • October: a transition month, with calm weather returning in the second half

Practical tips

Hour of day matters too. A sunrise visit between 5:30 and 7:30 AM brings fewer people and soft eastern light. Between 11 AM and 2 PM the public area gets busy and the overhead sun is harsh, so that is a good time to rest in shade.

Conclusion

If you value quiet water and space on Phu Quoc Khem Beach, the months from July to September are worth considering. Further reading: timing a visit to Khem Beach on Phu Quoc.
