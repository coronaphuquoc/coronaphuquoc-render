Spending Wisely on a Phu Quoc Hotel 5 Star Stay

A higher nightly rate is not the whole cost of a trip. On a large island, location affects how much you spend on taxis and transfers, so a lower-priced room far from your plans can end up costing more overall.

Good to know

Before choosing, list the places you expect to visit. If theme parks in the north are central to the trip, staying nearby may reduce daily transport. If the beach is the main goal, a resort on Long Beach could make sense.

Practical tips
