Catching Sunrise on the East-Facing Sand at Bai Sao

Sunset gets the attention on the west coast of Phu Quoc, but Bai Sao looks the other way. The shore faces east, so the first light rises straight out of the sea in front of you, with nothing in the way.

Timing the early start

Aim to stand on the sand at around 5:30 in the morning. That gives you the soft pastel phase before the sky turns gold. From Duong Dong the ride is roughly 30 to 40 minutes, so leave a little before five and allow extra minutes for the narrow lane down to the shore.

  • Check the sunrise time the night before, since it shifts through the year
  • Charge a head torch or phone light for the walk from the parking area
  • Wear sandals you can slip off at the waterline

Using the quiet hour

Before the day crowd arrives, the beach feels almost private. People use this window for a slow walk on cool sand, a few stretches or simply sitting with a coffee from the hotel. The water is usually flat at this hour, which makes a short swim pleasant once the light is up.

  • Keep voices low: fishermen are often working nearby
  • A wide-angle phone lens captures the curve between the two hills
  • Stay for breakfast seafood once the eateries start cooking

After the sun is up

By eight the heat builds, so this is a good moment to drive on to Khem Beach or An Thoi port rather than baking at midday. You will have had the calm part of the day to yourself.

Conclusion

An early alarm is all a sunrise at Bai Sao asks of you, and it often becomes a favourite memory of the trip. Plan the drive the evening before and leave room to linger. Further reading: Sao Beach location, food and tips.
