Hour by Hour: A November Day on Phu Quoc

One of the most useful ways to plan a November trip to Phu Quoc is to look at how a typical day unfolds. The weather follows a fairly reliable rhythm, and matching activities to it makes each day smoother.

Morning, 6 to 11 AM

Clear skies and temperatures of 24 to 27°C. This is the window for beach walks and exploring the national park before the heat builds.

Midday, 11 AM to 3 PM

Peak sunshine, with temperatures reaching 28 to 30°C. Good for water sports if you handle heat well, or for air-conditioned attractions if you prefer a break from the sun.

Afternoon, 3 to 6 PM

A brief shower is possible, with temperatures around 26 to 28°C. Indoor activities fit nicely here, as does a rest at your hotel.

Evening, from 6 PM

Cooler air at 24 to 26°C makes evenings pleasant for night markets and long dinners. The Dinh Cau Night Market is a lively choice.

Putting it together

  • Schedule hikes and photography early.
  • Keep swimming and snorkeling for late morning or midday.
  • Save museums, shows or a spa visit for mid-afternoon.
  • Go out again after sunset.

Why the rhythm matters

Most showers in November arrive in short bursts late in the day. Following this pattern means you rarely lose time to rain and you avoid the hottest part of the day for strenuous activities.

Conclusion

Planning by time of day turns November's mild weather into an advantage. Mornings for nature, middays for the water, afternoons for cover and evenings for food make a simple, effective routine. Further reading: the daily weather rhythm in November.
